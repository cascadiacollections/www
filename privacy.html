Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026. This page describes the studio website at cascadiacollections.com.

The website

These are static pages about the studio and its work. You can read them and follow links to the projects. The site does not ask you to sign in, and it has no forms.

The pages do not run analytics or advertising, and they do not embed third-party content. They do not set cookies.

If you dismiss the seasonal notice on the home page, the browser remembers that in localStorage, under the key cc-promo-bf2026. The value stays on your device. Clearing site data for this domain removes it.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the files. Cloudflare serves the domain, so a request reaches Cloudflare first. They receive the connection data needed to deliver the page: your IP address, the URL, and standard browser headers. Cloudflare may also apply its own security checks. We do not add tracking of our own.

Cloudflare describes its handling of that data in the Cloudflare privacy policy. GitHub describes theirs in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The apps

Data inside an app is covered by that app's own page:

  • ShoutKit for iPhone
  • ShoutKit for Android, the free build
  • ShoutKit for Android on Google Play
